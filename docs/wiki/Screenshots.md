## Main Application Features

### 1. Dashboard

<p align="center">
  <img
    src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/4a9ccb2c-aacd-4597-af2c-f58bea1767f6"
    alt="FinZen Architecture Diagram"
    width="900"

</p>

The Dashboard provides a quick overview of the user's financial status. It displays the total account balance, monthly income and expenses, recent transactions, and a chart showing how expenses are distributed across activities.

### 2. Transaction Management

<p align="center">
  <img
    src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/0bbd34b3-8f77-4a22-a80a-82b1ce2c4ee0"
    alt="FinZen Architecture Diagram"
    width="900"
  />
</p>

The Transactions section allows users to create, view, filter, edit, and delete their financial movements. Transactions can be organized by account, activity, type, month, and date range, making it easier to understand where money comes from and where it is spent.

### 3. Financial Reports

<p align="center">
  <img
    src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/73aa3e61-5f2f-4ce6-84e0-c670f16933c7"
    alt="FinZen Architecture Diagram"
    width="900"
  />
</p>

The Reports section transforms financial data into useful insights. Users can review income, expenses, net balance, monthly trends, budget performance, and savings-goal progress through summary cards, charts, and detailed tables.
